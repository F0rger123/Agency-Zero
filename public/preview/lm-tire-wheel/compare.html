What changed

Why it sells more.

Your 2,127 reviews do the selling

TodayYour 4.9 stars don't appear anywhere on the homepage.
NewYour rating in the first screen, plus a full section of real customer reviews that name Jake, Amy and the owner.

Your builds are the hero, not a flyer

TodayA flat banner graphic with the words baked into the picture, which Google can't read and gets blurry on phones.
NewYour own Tacoma in front of the shop, crisp headline text, and a 20-photo gallery of builds that opens full screen.

A shop that feels like L&M

TodayA stock store template that looks like hundreds of other tire sites.
NewWheels, tires and apparel in your style: filters, tire-size search, a set-of-4 cart, Affirm estimates and a 1-minute checkout that confirms fitment.

Built for thumbs

Today79 links and buttons too small to tap comfortably on a phone, and a chat pop-up covering your intro.
NewA Call / Directions / Shop bar that follows you down the page, big buttons, and a live "Open now" status.

Every service, bookable

TodayServices are a list of paragraphs. To book, customers have to find the contact page.
NewA services page with a booking form (vehicle, service, preferred day) that knows you're closed on weekends.

Financing that actually explains itself

TodayFinancing is a short Affirm box with a link out, and no idea what a set of tires would cost per month.
NewHow it works in 3 steps, a payment calculator, an FAQ, and "from $X/mo" on every product and cart.

One story, told the same way

TodayThe homepage says "36 years" and "34 years"; the About page says "since 1987". Images are labeled just "Tire", "Wheel", or broken code.
New"Family owned since 1987" everywhere, and every photo described properly for Google and for screen readers.

Ready for Google Maps searches

TodayHours and address sit at the bottom of a long page.
NewHours, address, map and the towns you serve (York, Hanover, Gettysburg…) up front, and built into the page data Google reads.
