Questions

Financing FAQ.

Does checking my rate affect my credit?

No. Affirm does a soft check to see if you qualify, which doesn't affect your credit score.

What can I finance?

Wheels, tires, lift kits and the install. Build your package online or with us at the shop, and we'll send one Affirm link for the confirmed total.

Are there late fees or hidden costs?

Affirm shows the total you'll pay, including interest, before you agree. There are no late fees or hidden costs.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes. You can pay off your Affirm loan early at any time with no penalty.

Can I still just pay at the shop?

Of course. Pick "Pay at the shop" at checkout and pay by card or cash when you pick up or after your install.
